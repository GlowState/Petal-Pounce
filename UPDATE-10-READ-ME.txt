PETAL POUNCE — UPDATE 10: SMOOTH ANALOG STEERING + CABINET TRIM

Install:
• Back up your current repository.
• Copy the included files to the matching paths in your existing project.
• Keep your other existing files; this ZIP is an update, not a standalone game.
• If your site uses root paths, copy the root files. If it uses dist/, copy dist/ files.
• Do not erase other assets (style.css, scenery.mjs, profile.mjs, etc.).

Included improvements:
• Knob face mathematically centered in the joystick ring.
• Slower proportional steering; slow on small tilt, fast on full left/right tilt.
• More forgiving top/bottom neutral cones; diagonal steering intentionally slower.
• Smooth acceleration/reversals instead of constant acceleration to full speed.
• Narrow, decorative arcade-machine trim on left/right edge of game screen.
• Existing mixed thorns, Fly control, and arcade text retained.

Rollback:
Use the accompanying Update 10 rollback ZIP (Update 9 files).
Both bundles are patches, not full standalone game archives.

Test on your phone: hold Fly and move the stick slowly; reverse over the top or
bottom; check screen-edge trim doesn't block gameplay. Cached versions may
require closing/reopening the game after GitHub Pages publishes.
