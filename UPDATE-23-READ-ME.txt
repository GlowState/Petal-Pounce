WHAT CHANGED

LATEST REVISION

• Fixed milestone celebrations not appearing reliably at 25 and 50 flowers.
• Replaced the canvas-only notice with a dedicated full-playfield celebration
  layer above the HUD, including a large bloom card and visible sparkle burst.
• Added a second toast announcement as a fallback, plus assertive screen-reader
  status text. The score bonus and permanent Best Flowers record are unchanged.
• Milestone cards now stay visible for 2.6 seconds and can restart cleanly if
  more than one milestone event is received.
• Updated the offline cache to v51 so mobile browsers fetch the corrected UI.

• Added flower milestones every 25 collected flowers. They celebrate without
  pausing the endless run and do not restore lives.
• Milestone score bonuses are +100 at 25 flowers, +200 at 50, +300 at 75,
  +400 at 100 and +500 at 125 flowers and every 25-flower marker beyond it.
• Added a brief hand-drawn milestone card, flower burst, arcade chime and
  supported-device haptic. Reduced Motion keeps the celebration stationary.
• Added a permanent Best Flowers in One Run record. The title and game-over
  screens show it, including a special callout when a new flower record is set.
• Increased maximum horizontal speed by exactly 3%: 216 to 222.48 pixels/sec.
• Reduced maximum climbing speed from 230 to 220 pixels/sec and maximum falling
  speed from 340 to 326 pixels/sec while preserving responsive acceleration.
• Preserved the pacing director, player-controlled camera and safe revival.
• Updated the offline cache to v50.

• Added an invisible pacing director with four repeating beats: calm, build,
  challenge and recovery. These are not levels and never interrupt the run.
• Challenge beats concentrate longer, thicker garden islands, precision gates
  and beetle patrol pressure. Recovery beats deliberately reduce all three.
• Existing altitude difficulty still increases hazard pressure over the full run;
  pacing now adds short-term rhythm so intensity does not feel constant.
• Every generated obstacle still clears the verified flower-to-flower route.
  Beetles keep permanent escape corridors and recovery sections reject new winds.
• Reduced the bee's maximum horizontal speed from 235 to 216 pixels per second
  (about 8%). Full-width leaf jumps receive extra vertical reaction space.
• Preserved the player-controlled camera: there is still no automatic scrolling.
• Updated the offline cache to v49.

• Added Bloom Streak scoring. Flowers 1–4 score ×1, 5–11 score ×2,
  12–24 score ×3 and 25+ score ×4. A miss or lost life resets the streak.
• Multipliers affect run score only. Shop flower currency is unchanged.
• Added one-time close-call bonuses: narrowly clearing an island or beetle
  awards +5 points, a small sound, floating message and supported-device haptic.
  Cooldowns and per-hazard flags prevent hovering beside danger to farm points.
• Upgraded the game-over summary with final score, flowers, longest streak,
  close-call count and either a new-best celebration or points-to-best message.
• Preserved the player-paced camera, safe revival and double-life protections.
• Updated the offline cache to v48.

• Removed automatic screen scrolling completely. Hovering no longer moves the
  course and the player controls the pace of the run again.
• The camera now follows only the highest point the bee has actually reached,
  eases smoothly upward after a climb and never drifts back down.
• Falling below the already-climbed playfield can still cost a life, but the
  camera itself never pushes the bee off screen.
• Fixed a double-life bug: a hazard could revive the bee at an old leaf that
  had already scrolled below the screen, immediately triggering a second loss.
• Revivals now keep the last touched leaf when it is visible and safe. If it is
  no longer usable, the game selects the nearest visible leaf with clearance
  from rocks and beetles—or the clearest open point when no leaf is visible—
  then grants 2.75 seconds of protection.
• The bottom screen boundary respects revival protection.
• Updated the offline cache to v47.

• Removed the on-screen distance readout. The center HUD now shows a clean,
  three-digit flower counter, and the duplicate flower display was removed.
• Removed distance from the game-over summary while keeping altitude internally
  for procedural difficulty, hazard density, winds and beetle progression.

• Beetles now patrol about 25–30% faster, with a safe maximum horizontal
  speed of 155 pixels per second. They still fly only horizontally and retain
  their permanent escape corridors.

• Lose 10 run points when an uncollected bloom scrolls below the playfield.
  Each missed flower is counted once and removed from the run. The score can
  go below zero. The flower bank is separate from the run score.
• A brief on-screen notice shows the points lost. Personal best uses the score
  at game over or when you return to the title.

BEETLE PATROLS

• Added hand-drawn rose-colored beetles with shell details, legs and flying wings.
• Beetles patrol horizontally at fixed heights and reverse direction smoothly.
• Patrol density increases with altitude. The first patrol is above 35 meters.
• Every patrol reserves a 105-pixel escape corridor and clears rocks, flowers
  and respawn leaves. Maximum horizontal patrol speed is 155 pixels per second.
• Beetle collisions cost one life and use the existing automatic revival.

• Replaced the purple thorn-bar artwork with hand-drawn floating garden islands.
• Added irregular moss caps, layered soil and stone, hanging roots, rock cracks,
  varied sharp points and tiny sprouts that fit the existing garden artwork.
• Varied island thickness, rock sizes and detail spacing so formations feel
  organic and hand-drawn instead of like repeating mechanical platforms.
• Touching hazard pieces on the same row now render as one continuous island,
  so dense sections look intentional instead of like separate bars.
• Edge-grown hazards appear rooted beyond the screen instead of ending abruptly.
• Added moving hazards to the island course for additional difficulty.
• Updated game labels, accessibility text and instructions to say thorn islands.
• Renamed the run HUD from HONEY to SCORE. Flowers add 10 score points each.
• Moved every difficulty notice into the center HUD to keep the playfield clear.
• Clipped island art to the original obstacle shapes and added a dashed ring
  around the bee during temporary protection.
• Used Flower Garden's smooth bee rendering across all gardens.

GAMEPLAY SAFETY

Patrols are checked against rocks, flowers and respawn leaves. Every patrol has
an escape corridor. Neighboring patrols also allow time to steer between their
escape openings. Beetle hits trigger automatic revival and brief protection.

FALLBACK

Petal-Pounce-Rollback-v48-Replay-Loop.zip. That rollback is the exact build
from immediately before this update.

After uploading, refresh the GitHub Pages site. If an older cached build appears,
close the tab completely and reopen it once.

LATEST UPDATE — RECORDS, ACHIEVEMENTS & BEE REWARDS (v52)

• Added RECORDS and ACHIEVEMENTS buttons side by side directly below LET’S FLY.
• Records now preserve personal best, most flowers, longest streak, most close
  calls, lifetime flowers collected and total runs played on this device.
• Added seven permanent achievements for flower milestones, clean collection,
  no-damage flying, clean high-wind survival, close calls and a ×4 streak.
• Each achievement unlocks a hand-drawn bee color or trail. Rewards are purely
  cosmetic and never change speed, lift, collision size, score or difficulty.
• Added in-game achievement banners, progress readouts and equip buttons.
• The Classic bee remains available at all times.
• Older saved accomplishments are recognized where existing records can prove
  them; new challenge-specific progress begins with this update.
• Updated the offline cache to v52.
