# Content rating and audience draft

## IARC/content questionnaire notes

- Violence: A cartoon bee can touch thorny vines, disappear briefly, and revive. There is no blood, gore, injury detail, human violence, or realistic weapon use.
- Fear/horror: None.
- Sexual content/nudity: None.
- Language: None.
- Gambling: None.
- Controlled substances: None.
- User-generated content or communication: None.
- Location sharing: None.
- Purchases inside the app: None.

Expected result: a low/everyone-style content rating, subject to the official questionnaire.

## Target audience recommendation

The game is mechanically appropriate for ages 6 and up and does not contain ads, tracking, social features, purchases inside the app, or external links in normal gameplay. Select the actual age groups honestly in Play Console and complete any Families declarations Google presents.
