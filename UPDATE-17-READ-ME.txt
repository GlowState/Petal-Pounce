PETAL POUNCE — UPDATE 17: ALTITUDE RHYTHM GENERATOR
==================================================

THIS IS A CUMULATIVE UPDATE
You only need this ZIP. It includes Update 16 and the new Update 17 files.

INSTALL
1. Replace the four files in your repository root:
   index.html, engine.mjs, game.mjs, sw.js
2. Replace the same four files inside the dist folder.
3. Commit and push. GitHub Pages may take a few minutes to refresh.
4. On iPhone, close the old tab and reopen the site. The new v32 service-worker
   cache is designed to replace the older cached build.

GENERATOR LOOP
1. Read the total altitude and select the active difficulty band.
2. Scale climb/scroll speed, wave spacing, item ratio, hazard ratio, and the
   allowed hazard streak from that band.
3. Use an error-accumulation “hazard debt” to converge on the requested ratio
   without producing unstructured coin-flip randomness.
4. Schedule a readable 3–5 item sine-wave flower chain.
5. When a hazard is due, insert a speed-scaled reaction buffer equal to at
   least 1.5 bee lengths, then choose no more than two obstacle lanes.
6. Preserve a reachable open lane. The safe route may move at most one lane
   per row, preventing instant impossible zig-zags.
7. Convert the validated row plan into leaves, flowers, and fully horizontal
   thorn hitboxes. Append the next chunk above the current world.

DIFFICULTY TABLE
0–200m:    75% items / 25% hazards, 1.0x speed, max streak 1
201–600m:  65% items / 35% hazards, 1.25x speed, max streak 2
601–1200m: 55% items / 45% hazards, 1.5x speed, max streak 3
1201–2000m:45% items / 55% hazards, 1.8x speed, max streak 4
2001m+:    40% items / 60% hazards, 2.0x speed, max streak 5

PATTERNS
- Flower Chain: 3–5 flowers/leaves following a smooth three-lane sine path.
- Hazard Wall: two thorn lanes immediately after the chain crest/trough.
- Bait Trap: a glowing flower worth 3 flowers/30 score behind a two-lane wall.

FAIRNESS GUARANTEES
- Every generated row has at least one obstacle-free lane.
- A row contains no more than two obstacles.
- All generated thorns are horizontal.
- Reaction distance expands in direct proportion to speed, preserving the
  same reaction time at 1.0x through 2.0x.
- Wasps avoid newly generated hazard rows and scale with altitude.
