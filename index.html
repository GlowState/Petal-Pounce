THE FIELD GUIDE

Find your wings.

Climb through an endless retro garden run. Collect every honey pot in a section to roll straight into the next one, and keep going with the lives you have left. Your chosen background stays equipped.

JOYSTICK

Guide the stick to steerDrag the joystick left or right for smoother movement. You can steer and hold Fly at the same time. Keyboard: ← → or A / D.

SPACE / ↑

Hold to flyRelease to descend. Leaves give you a bounce and a safe place to return.

3 LIVES

Carry your lives forwardEach cleared section adds one life, up to three. The next section starts immediately. Lose a life and you revive automatically at your last leaf, with brief protection.

GARDEN SHOP

Pick a permanent gardenEvery honey pot adds one to your bank. Twilight and Classic are free. Unlock Flower Garden for 60 pots or Glowwood for 120. Equip any owned garden for all your runs. Unlocks and honey pots are saved on this device.

P / ESC

Take a breatherThe game pauses when you switch away.

Each honey pot also earns 10 honey for your run score. Clearing a section awards a bonus and can earn you a life. Banked honey pots and unlocked gardens survive losing a life, game over, and starting a new run. A new run starts with three lives.
