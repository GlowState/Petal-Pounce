THE FIELD GUIDE

Find your wings.

Fly upward through one endless garden with no levels or finish line. The camera moves only when you climb. Gather flowers, dodge sharp floating islands, and watch for faster beetles patrolling from side to side. Beetle encounters become more frequent as you climb. Occasional high-wind scenes gently push the bee left or right.

JOYSTICK

Tilt in any directionLeft/right steers, up/down fine-tunes your glide (up = more lift, down = quicker drop). Keep holding Fly to climb. Your thumb can travel outside the joystick without losing control. Steering responds to left or right tilt at any angle, including near the top or bottom. Tilt farther for more speed; diagonal steering is slightly gentler. The enlarged touch area makes thumb repositioning easier. Release to center. Keyboard: ← → or A / D.

SPACE / ↑

Hold to flyRelease to descend. Leaves give you a bounce and a safe place to return.

3 LIVES

Carry your lives forwardStart with three lives. There are no extra lives or level resets. Lose a life and you revive automatically at your last leaf, with brief protection.

RHYTHM

Read the gardenLeaves and flowers can appear anywhere across the screen. Mossy islands, edge-grown ledges, rock-tooth gates, dense formations and calm stretches create a changing route, but the flower path always remains reachable.

STREAKS

Chain every bloomCollect 5 flowers without a miss or hit for ×2 score, 12 for ×3 and 25 for ×4. The multiplier changes score only—every collected bloom keeps its normal shop value.

MILESTONES

Reach the next markerEvery 25 collected flowers triggers a celebration and score bonus without pausing the run. Bonuses grow from +100 at 25 flowers to +500 at 125 flowers and beyond. Milestones never restore lives or multiply your flower bank.

CLOSE CALL

Brush past dangerPass just outside an island or beetle hitbox for a one-time +5 bonus. The same hazard cannot be farmed repeatedly.

CLIMB

You set the paceThe screen follows only the highest point you reach and never moves upward on its own. The garden naturally shifts through calmer stretches, building pressure, short challenges and recovery space. If you fall completely below the screen after climbing, you lose one life and revive automatically at a visible safe point.

HIGH WINDS

Counter-steer the gustWind cannot appear before 450m and remains uncommon early in a run. Calm periods shorten gradually with distance, but occasional extra-long calm breaks can happen anywhere. After “Caution: High Winds” appears, you have three seconds to prepare for a 20–30 second left or right push.

FLOWER SHOP

Pick a permanent gardenEvery normal bloom adds one flower to your bank. Twilight and Classic are free. Unlock Flower Garden for 60 flowers or Glowwood for 120. Equip any owned garden for every run. Unlocks and flowers are saved on this device.

RECORDS

Build your garden legacyRecords, achievements, unlocked bee styles and equipped rewards are saved on this device. Cosmetic bee rewards never change gameplay.

P / ESC

Take a breatherThe game pauses when you switch away.

Pick a flower for at least 10 points. Bloom Streak multipliers raise run score only. If a bloom slips below the screen as you climb, you lose 10 points, the streak resets and the flower is gone for that run. Your score can go below zero. Banked flowers and unlocked gardens carry over between runs.

YOUR FIRST FLIGHT

Four things. Then fly.

Petal Pounce is easy to learn and meant to get tougher naturally as you climb.

1 · STEER

Tilt the joystick left or right. Tilting up or down gently adjusts your glide.

2 · FLY

Hold the large wing button to rise. Release it to descend onto leaves.

3 · SURVIVE

You have three lives. Leaves become safe revival points after you touch them.

4 · COLLECT

Flowers build score and streaks. Letting one pass costs 10 points and breaks the streak.
