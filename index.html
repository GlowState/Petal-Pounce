THE FIELD GUIDE

Find your wings.

Fly upward through one endless garden with no levels or finish line. The screen keeps climbing, so keep the bee above the bottom edge. Gather flowers, dodge sharp floating islands, and watch for faster beetles patrolling from side to side. Beetle encounters become more frequent as you climb. Occasional high-wind scenes gently push the bee left or right.

JOYSTICK

Tilt in any directionLeft/right steers, up/down fine-tunes your glide (up = more lift, down = quicker drop). Keep holding Fly to climb. Your thumb can travel outside the joystick without losing control. Steering responds to left or right tilt at any angle, including near the top or bottom. Tilt farther for more speed; diagonal steering is slightly gentler. The enlarged touch area makes thumb repositioning easier. Release to center. Keyboard: ← → or A / D.

SPACE / ↑

Hold to flyRelease to descend. Leaves give you a bounce and a safe place to return.

3 LIVES

Carry your lives forwardStart with three lives. There are no extra lives or level resets. Lose a life and you revive automatically at your last leaf, with brief protection.

RHYTHM

Read the gardenLeaves and flowers can appear anywhere across the screen. Mossy islands, edge-grown ledges, rock-tooth gates, dense formations and calm stretches create a changing route, but the flower path always remains reachable.

KEEP UP

The garden never waitsThe screen rises continuously during active play and never scrolls back down. Fall completely below the bottom edge and you lose one life, then revive automatically at a visible safe point.

HIGH WINDS

Counter-steer the gustWind cannot appear before 450m and remains uncommon early in a run. Calm periods shorten gradually with distance, but occasional extra-long calm breaks can happen anywhere. After “Caution: High Winds” appears, you have three seconds to prepare for a 20–30 second left or right push.

FLOWER SHOP

Pick a permanent gardenEvery normal bloom adds one flower to your bank. Twilight and Classic are free. Unlock Flower Garden for 60 flowers or Glowwood for 120. Equip any owned garden for every run. Unlocks and flowers are saved on this device.

P / ESC

Take a breatherThe game pauses when you switch away.

Pick a flower for 10 points. If its bloom slips below the screen as you climb, you lose 10 points and the flower is gone for that run. Your score can go below zero. Banked flowers and unlocked gardens carry over between runs.
