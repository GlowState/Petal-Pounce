How to Play

Tap any topic to see how it works.

✥JOYSTICK
Steer and glide

Tilt the joystick left or right to steer. Tilting upward adds a little lift; tilting downward helps you descend. Lean farther to turn faster. Your thumb can move beyond the visible joystick while you hold it. Keyboard: ← / → or A / D.

✧FLY BUTTON
Climb and descend

Hold Fly to climb. Release to fall toward a leaf. Adjust your direction and glide with the joystick to land safely. On a keyboard, hold Space or ↑ to fly.

✿FLOWERS & SCORE
Collect blooms

Collect flowers to increase your score and your saved flower balance. A missed flower costs 10 points and breaks your streak. Saved flowers can be used for cosmetic items in Flower Shop.

♡LIVES & CHECKPOINTS
Three chances

Each run begins with three lives. Touching a leaf sets a safe return point. When you lose a life, your bee returns to the latest safe checkpoint with brief protection. Losing all three lives ends the run.

⚠LEAVES & HAZARDS
Choose your route

Land on leaves to rest and bounce. Steer around thorn-covered islands and moving beetles. The course changes as you climb; the route is designed to be navigable, even through harder stretches.

✦BLOOM STREAKS
Build your multiplier

Collect flowers without missing one or taking a hit to build a streak: 5 flowers earns ×2, 12 earns ×3, and 25 earns ×4 score. A multiplier changes points, not the number of flowers added to your shop balance.

★MILESTONES
Celebrate every 25

Every 25 flowers collected in a run triggers a milestone celebration and points bonus. Milestones do not restore lives or multiply saved flowers.

◇CLOSE CALLS
Earn risk-reward points

Fly close to an obstacle or beetle without touching it to earn a one-time +5 close-call bonus. You cannot repeatedly earn points from the same hazard.

≋HIGH WINDS
Watch for the warning

A warning gives you a few seconds before a stronger left or right gust. Counter-steer with the joystick until the wind settles. Gusts become possible later in a run, with calmer stretches between them.

▣FLOWER SHOP
Unlock cosmetics

Visit Flower Shop to unlock Levels, Bees, and Trails with your saved flowers. Choose owned designs in Customize. Achievement reward bees remain tied to their original challenges.

♜HIGH SCORES & ACHIEVEMENTS
Track your progress

Open High Scores from the main menu. Use the Records and Achievements tabs to see your best runs, challenges, and unlocked bee styles. Cosmetics do not change performance.

ⅡPAUSE & RETURN
Take a break

Tap Pause to stop the run, then resume when ready. The game also pauses when you switch away. You can return to the main menu from the pause screen.

YOUR FIRST FLIGHT

Four things. Then fly.

Petal Pounce is easy to learn and meant to get tougher naturally as you climb.

1 · STEER

Tilt the joystick left or right. Tilting up or down gently adjusts your glide.

2 · FLY

Hold the large wing button to rise. Release it to descend onto leaves.

3 · SURVIVE

You have three lives. Leaves become safe revival points after you touch them.

4 · COLLECT

Flowers build score and streaks. Letting one pass costs 10 points and breaks the streak.
