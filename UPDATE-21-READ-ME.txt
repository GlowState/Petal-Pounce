PETAL POUNCE — UPDATE 21: HIGH WINDS
====================================

THIS IS A CUMULATIVE UPDATE
Install this directly over Update 20. Earlier updates are not required.

INSTALL
1. Replace index.html, engine.mjs, game.mjs, and sw.js in the repository root.
2. Replace those same four files inside the dist folder.
3. Commit and push to GitHub.
4. Close and reopen the game on iPhone so the revised v37 cache can activate.

HIGH-WIND OBSTACLE SCENES
- High-wind scenes cannot begin before the player reaches 450 meters.
- Reaching 450m arms the system, then starts a separate randomized 35–70
  second calm window. Wind never fires immediately at the altitude threshold.
- A message displays exactly: “Caution: High Winds”.
- The player receives a full three-second preparation warning.
- Each scene randomly pushes either left or right.
- Each active wind sequence lasts a randomized 20–30 seconds.
- Wind strength uses soft layered gusts rather than a perfectly constant shove.
- Maximum wind drift remains far below normal steering speed, so holding the
  joystick against the wind always overpowers it.
- Later altitude bands increase wind strength only slightly.
- Calm timing scales with the player's total altitude:
    450–600m:    120–190 seconds
    601–1200m:   100–160 seconds
    1201–2000m:  80–135 seconds
    2001m+:      65–110 seconds
- Every cooldown has a 22% chance to receive an additional 45–90 second
  extra-long calm period.
- Wind timing continues through the short automatic-revival animation, but
  pauses normally when the player pauses or leaves the app.

VISUAL AND AUDIO TELEGRAPHING
- Animated wind streaks move in the push direction.
- An on-screen WIND arrow shows the current direction.
- Warning, wind-start, and wind-end cues have distinct subtle sounds.
- Wind-start text identifies whether the bee is being pushed left or right.
- “WINDS CALM” confirms the scene has ended.
- Reduced-motion mode uses fewer, slower wind streaks.

VALIDATION
- 10/10 automated tests passed.
- Wind events verified to warn before applying force.
- Durations verified between 20 and 30 seconds.
- Both push directions verified across deterministic seeds.
- Counter-steering verified to overpower maximum wind drift.
- Ten-minute simulations averaged 3.26 wind scenes at 500m, 3.76 at 900m,
  4.22 at 1500m, and 4.76 at 2500m.
- Wind occupied about 13% of early simulated play and about 20% at the hard
  cap, leaving the majority of every run calm.
- Calm gaps ranged from approximately two minutes early to just over one
  minute at the hard cap, with extra-long breaks extending beyond those ranges.
- Existing generator, thorn-wall, solvability, and route-clearance tests pass.
