## Full description

Small wings. Wild gardens. One more run.

Guide a determined little bee through an endless climb of leaves, flowers, and thorny vines. Hold the wing button to fly, steer between hazards, and collect every bloom to clear the garden.

Every garden is a fresh challenge. Leaves shift, vines change size and position, and the climb gets longer as your run continues. You begin with three lives, revive automatically after a hit, and earn a life whenever you clear a garden—up to the three-life maximum.

COLLECT AND CUSTOMIZE

Every flower you collect is saved to your flower bank. Use those flowers to permanently unlock new garden backgrounds, each with its own atmosphere and flower design. Equip any garden you own and keep it for every level.

BUILT FOR QUICK ARCADE RUNS

• Simple left, right, and fly controls
• Endless procedurally arranged gardens
• Random leaf layouts and horizontal thorn hazards
• Automatic revival and leaf checkpoints
• Three lives that carry between gardens
• Permanent flower bank and background unlocks
• Four distinct garden themes
• Offline play
• No ads, accounts, analytics, or tracking
• One purchase—no additional payments

How far can your wings take you?
