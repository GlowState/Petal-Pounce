PETAL POUNCE — UPDATE 19: CENTER THORN PRESSURE
================================================

THIS IS A CUMULATIVE UPDATE
Install this directly over Update 18. Earlier updates are not required.

INSTALL
1. Replace index.html, engine.mjs, game.mjs, and sw.js in the repository root.
2. Replace the same four files inside the dist folder.
3. Commit and push to GitHub.
4. Close and reopen the game on iPhone so the v34 cache can activate.

WHAT CHANGED
- The center lane is no longer a reliable straight path upward.
- Center-lane hazard pressure now increases with altitude:
    0–200m:     65% center pressure
    201–600m:   70% center pressure
    601–1200m:  75% center pressure
    1201–2000m: 80% center pressure
    2001m+:     85% center pressure target
- The generator uses an accumulator instead of a random coin flip, preventing
  long stretches where the center accidentally remains clear.
- More hazard rows use two thorn vines, increasing visible vine density.
- Center vines are approximately 12% thicker and can become heavy sooner.
- Center blocks deliberately open a reachable left or right escape.
- Side openings alternate when the player can physically reach the other side.
- The safe route still moves no more than one lane per row.
- No row ever seals all three lanes.
- Existing hazard density and slower Update 18 speed curve are preserved.
- Random leaf positions, flower chains, hazard walls, and risky bait traps are
  preserved.
- Help text now explains that the middle lane is actively pressured.
- Cache version increased to v34.

VALIDATION
- 8/8 automated generator tests passed.
- Hundreds of seeds verified for ratios, reaction buffers, lane coverage,
  random leaf placement, center pressure, and deterministic generation.
- Ten-chunk runtime smoke test produced 333 thorn vines across 174 hazard rows.
- The middle lane was blocked on 81% of those runtime hazard rows.
- Zero sealed rows and zero diagonal thorn vines were detected.

ROLLBACK
Use Petal-Pounce-Rollback-Update-18-Hazard-Density.zip to return to the exact
pre-Update-19 release.
