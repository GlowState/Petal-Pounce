PETAL POUNCE

Privacy policy.

Updated November 10, 2026

Petal Pounce does not collect, transmit, sell, or share player personal information through its game features. The game has no accounts, ads, analytics, social features, tracking SDKs, or payments inside the game.

Progress saved locally

Your high scores, flower balance, achievements, unlocked and equipped bees, levels and trails, sound settings, and Fly/Joystick appearance preferences are stored on your device. The browser version may cache game code and artwork for offline play. Your progress is not sent to the game publisher.

Website typography

The web version may request the Jersey 20 typeface from Google Fonts while online. Google may receive network information such as your IP address for that font request. The Android app bundles the game for offline play and does not require this font connection.

Deleting progress

You can delete locally saved progress by clearing Petal Pounce site/app storage or uninstalling the Android app. There is no account or server-side player record for the publisher to erase.

Children's privacy

Petal Pounce does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults through its game features. If this changes, the policy and applicable disclosures will be updated before collection begins.

Changes and contact

Material changes will be posted here. For privacy questions, use the developer contact address shown on the Petal Pounce Google Play listing.
