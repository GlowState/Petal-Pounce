# Store screenshot plan

Capture screenshots from the installed Android internal-test build so every image accurately represents the submitted app.

Required minimum: two phone screenshots. Recommended: four portrait screenshots at 1080×1920 or another valid 9:16 size.

1. **Title screen** — Twilight Garden equipped; title and large controls visible.
2. **Active run** — HUD, three-life display, scattered leaves, and horizontal thorn vines visible.
3. **Garden shop** — all four backgrounds and their unique flowers visible.
4. **Life/revival or clear screen** — show automatic revival messaging or the earned-life garden-complete screen.

Do not add a phone frame, Play badge, unrelated logos, or imagery that is not in the game. Remove notifications and personal status-bar details before capture. Add accurate alt text in Play Console.
