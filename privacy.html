PETAL POUNCE

Privacy policy.

Effective November 1, 2026

Petal Pounce does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. The game has no player accounts, advertising, analytics, social features, or third-party tracking SDKs.

Data stored on your device

Your high score, sound preference, flower balance, unlocked gardens, and equipped garden are saved only in local storage on your device. A service worker may cache the game’s code and artwork so it can launch reliably and work offline. This data is not sent to the game publisher.

Data deletion

You can delete game data by clearing Petal Pounce site/app storage or uninstalling the Android app. Because no account or server record exists, the publisher has no remote player record to delete.

Children’s privacy

Petal Pounce does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults. If future versions add services that collect data, this policy and the in-app disclosure will be updated before those services are used.

Changes and contact

Material changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date. For privacy questions, use the developer contact address published on the Petal Pounce Google Play listing.
